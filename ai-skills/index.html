Stop re-explaining yourself to your AI

Your assistant isn't dumb. It just improvises a slightly different answer every time, because your standards live in your head. Skills are those standards, written down once in plain English, loaded automatically when your assistant recognizes the situation.

Paste it into Claude or Hermes. It asks what you want to be better at, shows you the few skills that fit, and waits for your yes before saving anything.

          What's in the library
        

        

          You don't need to pick from this list. The setup prompt asks what you want and
          suggests a few. This is just what's in there.
        

        

          

            Research and decisions
          

          

            When you need to know something, or decide something.
          

          

            

              
deep-dive

              

                “Go figure this out” comes back with a recommendation and what would
                change it, instead of a reading list.
              

              

                Consults several different AI models on Hermes; on Claude it uses Claude
                alone.
              

            

            

              
mob-check

              

                What real people are saying right now on Reddit, X, and YouTube, not the
                SEO version.
              

            

            

              
grok-search

              

                Live web and X search when it needs what happened today.
              

              

                needs env: XAI_API_KEY (xAI console)
              

            

            

              
multi-review

              

                Runs a draft, plan, or decision past several critical angles before you
                send it.
              

              

                Reviews with several different AI models on Hermes; on Claude, Claude
                reviews its own work.
              

            

          

        

        

          

            Getting work finished
          

          

            For work that drags on, stalls, or piles up.
          

          

            

              
keep-going

              

                It finishes the job instead of stopping to ask you which option you
                want.
              

            

            

              
project-steward

              

                Runs a portfolio of long projects like a chief of staff.
              

              

                Hermes only · needs env: TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN (for the living board)
              

            

            

              
email-steward

              

                Triages inboxes on a schedule and surfaces only what needs you.
              

              

                Hermes only · needs email CLI: gog or himalaya; Hermes cron + delegation
                toolsets enabled
              

            

            

              
address-pr-comments

              

                For engineers: triages code review feedback and fixes what’s valid.
              

              

                needs gh CLI, authenticated
              

            

            

              
pr-review-sweep

              

                Nightly sweep of merged PRs for unhandled review comments.
              

              

                Hermes only · needs gh CLI, authenticated; Hermes delegation toolset
                enabled
              

            

          

        

        

          
Making things

          

            Turning messy input into something you can hand to a person.
          

          

            

              
google-docs

              

                Turns messy notes into a real, formatted Google Doc.
              

              

                needs gog CLI, authorized via `gog auth login`
              

            

            

              
google-sheets

              

                Builds a real spreadsheet from raw data, formatted and ready to use.
              

              

                needs gog CLI, authorized for Google Sheets and Drive; python3; pdftoppm
                (poppler-utils), for multipage visual QA rasterization; uv, to run the
                XLSX verification snippets; openpyxl, via `uv run --with openpyxl` (not
                a standing install)
              

            

            

              
google-slides

              

                Turns an outline into a presentation deck.
              

              

                needs gog CLI, authorized via `gog auth login`; pandoc (for markdown
                conversion)
              

            

            

              
diagram-rendering

              

                Describe a diagram in words, get a real image back.
              

              

                needs chromium binary on PATH (or CHROMIUM_BIN) for local rasterize;
                network access to a Kroki host (KROKI_BASE) and QuickChart
                (QUICKCHART_BASE)
              

            

            

              
mini-app

              

                Runs the little web apps your agent serves.
              

              

                Hermes only · needs host services: Caddy + PM2; Tailscale Serve/Funnel

Hermes only

What it will and won't do

          
    
            
  • 
              It asks what you want first, then recommends a few skills rather than
              installing everything.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              It shows you each one in plain English, and where the files would live,
              before anything is saved.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              It waits for your yes, and won't replace anything you already have without
              showing you first.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Skills are plain-English text files, not software. You can read any of
              them before installing, and delete one at any time.
            
    • 
          

          

            The library installs into Claude and Hermes today, not ChatGPT.
          

          

            These are instructions your assistant follows, not enforced technical
            controls, so review the list it proposes before you approve it. Everything
            is public on
            GitHub.
